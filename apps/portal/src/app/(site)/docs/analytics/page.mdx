# How we count players

A run counts as a **play** once your game ends it, or once it reaches <span className="font-mono">{QUALIFIED_PLAY_MS / 1000} s</span> of active play, whichever comes first. A **player** is a person with at least one play. Every number on your dashboard follows from those two rules.

## Why not count instantly?

In a feed, a tap is cheap. Someone taps your card and swipes on half a second later: that's not a player, and counting them would inflate every number you see. A long threshold would be wrong too: a run that ends in two seconds because the player crashed into the first pipe is a real play. "Ended, or <span className="font-mono">{QUALIFIED_PLAY_MS / 1000} s</span> in" keeps both the quick death and the real attempt, and drops the accidental tap. Runs that never get there show up as *bounced starts*, so you can still see them.

## What counts as playtime

Only time spent in a run while your game was running. When the run is paused (a sheet opened, the app went to the background, the player was idle for 20 s), dragged by a feed swipe, or offscreen, the clock stops. The app reports this time to our servers, which never accept more than the real time that has passed.

## What never counts

- Your own plays, and plays by Thumbcade admins.
- On the public play count: plays past 100 by one person in one day. Your dashboard's plays follow the same rule.
- Plays in the web player on thumbcade.com. They don't open a server session yet.

## Milestones

| Milestone | When it lands |
| --- | --- |
| Players | Your game passes 100, 1,000, 10,000, 100,000 and 1,000,000 players |
| Playtime | 100, 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000 hours played |
| Firsts | Your first player, like, comment and challenge |
| Best day yet | A new record for players in one day, after a week live, with 50 or more players that day (at most once a week) |
| Taking off | Three times your usual daily players, with 200 or more that day |

Each milestone sends one notification (at most one a day per game) and comes with a card you can share. Nothing about a milestone is public until you share it.

## Freshness

Numbers update every 10 minutes. Days are UTC days. Return rates fill in once their window is over: day-1 return the day after tomorrow, day-7 return after a week.
