All launch-game art (icons, sprites, chess pieces, card faces, posters) and every sound effect are original to this repository: drawn in code or SVG, synthesized with WebAudio at runtime. No samples, sprite sheets or third-party art.

npm dependencies are listed in each package.json under their own licenses. Users see every third-party notice: the app under Settings → Open-source licences and the website at `/licenses` (footer: Licences), both listing the full production dependency closure, the fonts and their licence and notice texts. Both read checked-in files generated by `scripts/licenses/collect.ts`; regenerate with `pnpm --filter @thumbcade/mobile notices` and `pnpm --filter @thumbcade/portal notices` (the portal also regenerates on every build, and a test in each app fails while its file is stale). Each launch-game bundle lists the npm packages bundled into it, with their license texts, in `licenses/libraries.txt`; `docs/THIRD_PARTY.md` has the full picture.
